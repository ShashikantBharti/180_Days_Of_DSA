## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What is a Race Condition?
**Ans:** A situation where multiple processes access shared data concurrently, and the final result depends on the order of execution.

### Q2: Explain the Critical Section Problem.
**Ans:** The challenge of designing a protocol where processes can cooperate without causing data inconsistency or deadlocks.
