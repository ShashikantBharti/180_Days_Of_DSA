## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What is the Banker's Algorithm?
**Ans:** A resource allocation and deadlock avoidance algorithm that tests for safety by simulating the allocation for predetermined maximum possible amounts of all resources.

### Q2: How to recover from deadlock?
**Ans:** Abort all deadlocked processes, or abort them one by one, or preempt resources (rollback).
