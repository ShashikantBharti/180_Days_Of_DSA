## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What is Seek Time?
**Ans:** The time taken for the disk arm to move to the specific track containing the data.

### Q2: Explain SSTF (Shortest Seek Time First) Scheduling.
**Ans:** Selects the request with the minimum seek time from the current head position. Can lead to starvation.
