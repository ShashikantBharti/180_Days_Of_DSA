## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What are the 4 Coffman conditions for deadlock?
**Ans:** Mutual Exclusion, Hold and Wait, No Preemption, Circular Wait. All 4 must exist for a deadlock to occur.

### Q2: Explain Deadlock Prevention.
**Ans:** Ensuring at least one of the 4 conditions is never met (e.g., requesting all resources at once).
