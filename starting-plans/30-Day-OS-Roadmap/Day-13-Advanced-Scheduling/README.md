---

## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What is Starvation?
**Ans:** A condition where a low-priority process never runs because higher-priority processes keep arriving.

### Q2: How does Aging solve starvation?
**Ans:** By gradually increasing the priority of processes that wait in the system for a long time.

### Q3: Difference between MLQ and MLFQ?
**Ans:** In MLQ, processes belong to fixed queues. In MLFQ, processes can move between queues based on their behavior (CPU-bound vs I/O-bound).
