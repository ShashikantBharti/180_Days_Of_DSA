## 💬 Interview Q&A
### Q1: What is the Convoy Effect?
**Ans:** Happens in FCFS when a long process blocks many small processes, leading to poor CPU utilization.

### Q2: Is SJF optimal?
**Ans:** Yes, for minimizing average waiting time, provided we know process durations in advance.
